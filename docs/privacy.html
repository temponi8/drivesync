Privacy policy

DriveSync · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

DriveSync runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it has no server of its own.

What DriveSync accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant DriveSync access to your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). It uses this access only to:

  • list, download and upload files in the single Drive folder you choose during setup, and
  • apply changes (create, update, rename, delete) so that folder matches the local folder on your computer.

It does not read or modify anything outside that folder, and it does not access any other Google service.

Where your data goes

  • Files travel directly between your computer and Google's servers over HTTPS. They are never sent to the DriveSync author or any third party.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer, in rclone's configuration file (~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf).
  • Sync logs and status are stored only on your computer (~/.local/state/drivesync/).
  • DriveSync contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Use of Google user data

DriveSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke DriveSync's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To remove all local DriveSync data, uninstall the program and delete ~/.config/drivesync, ~/.local/state/drivesync and the rclone remote (rclone config delete gdrive). Your files in Google Drive and in your local folder are not affected.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions can be asked by opening an issue on GitHub.
